# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Customers seeking barbershop services, local barbers, and barbershop owners/management.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Traditional barbershops rely on walk-ins and rigid, fixed-time booking slots that fail to adapt to varying service durations and peak-hour rush spikes, causing long, unpredictable wait times and schedule overlaps. Additionally, customers lack an efficient way to find and match with barbers based on specific style expertise, pricing, and availability.

## Why does it matter?
Poor queue management directly leads to customer frustration and lost bookings—costing shops up to 54% of preventable walk-outs—while difficulty in finding and booking the right stylist drives 57% of client departures, resulting in severe schedule delays, underutilized barbers, and lost business revenue.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Traditional barbershops suffer from inefficient queue management and rigid, fixed-interval booking systems that fail to handle fluctuating service times and peak-hour traffic spikes, leaving customers stuck with unpredictable wait times (Limlawan & Anussornnitisarn, 2021; Salah & Srinivas, 2022). Furthermore, customers struggle to find and book barbers who match their specific style preferences, schedules, and budgets, with key matching difficulties directly driving 57% of client departures (Sophy, 2024). This friction causes schedule overlaps, higher customer drop-off rates, and lost revenue for service providers. Without real-time queue tracking and intelligent barber-matching capabilities, barbershops cannot optimize daily throughput or maintain long-term customer retention.
