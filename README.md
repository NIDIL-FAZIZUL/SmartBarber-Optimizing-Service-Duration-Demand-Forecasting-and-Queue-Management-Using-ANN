# Smart Barbershop Queue Management & Barber-Matching System

A dynamic queue forecasting and intelligent barber-matching platform designed to eliminate unpredictable wait times and pair customers with their ideal stylist.

## Problem statement
Traditional barbershops suffer from inefficient queue management and rigid, fixed-interval booking systems that fail to handle fluctuating service times and peak-hour traffic spikes, leaving customers stuck with unpredictable wait times (Limlawan & Anussornnitisarn, 2021; Salah & Srinivas, 2022). Furthermore, customers struggle to find and book barbers who match their specific style preferences, schedules, and budgets, with key matching difficulties directly driving 57% of client departures (Sophy, 2024). This friction causes schedule overlaps, higher customer drop-off rates, and lost revenue for service providers. Without real-time queue tracking and intelligent barber-matching capabilities, barbershops cannot optimize daily throughput or maintain long-term customer retention.
